# Privacy Policy — Walletly

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Walletly ("the app") is developed by Abdul Aleem. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary
**Your financial data stays on your phone.** The app has no account, no login and no server. We do not collect, store, sell or share the financial data you enter. The only data that leaves your device is described below (rate downloads, and purchase verification if you buy Pro).

## Data stored on your device
Everything you enter — transactions, gold, silver and other assets, transfers, loans (including the names of people you lend to or borrow from), favourites, categories, accounts (name and optionally the last 4 digits of a card or account number), budgets, bills and subscriptions, trips, zakat settings and payments, repeating transactions, savings goals, receipt photos you choose to keep, and settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Full card or account numbers are never requested or stored.

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore). Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by your phone; the app never receives your biometric data.

## Backups
Backups are only created when you ask for them (Export, or Automatic backup in Settings). Receipt photos are not included in backups. They are saved as files to a folder **you choose** on your device, or shared through an app **you choose**. We never receive a copy. A normal backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. You can also create a **password-protected backup**: the file is encrypted on your phone (AES-256, with a key made from your password) before it is saved or shared. We never receive the password and cannot recover it, so if you forget it the file cannot be opened.

## Internet use
The app connects to the internet only to download currency exchange rates and gold/silver prices from public services (open.er-api.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.dev). These requests contain no personal or financial information. These services may log standard technical data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

## Camera, receipt scanning and receipt photos
If you choose to scan a receipt, the app uses the camera (or a photo you pick) and reads the text **on your device** using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The text is not uploaded and not sent to us or anyone else. If you attach a photo to a transaction, or keep the photo from a scan, it is saved in the app's private storage on your device, is never uploaded, and is deleted when you remove it or delete the transaction. If you don't keep a photo, only the amount, date and shop name you choose to save are kept.

## Exports
When you export your transactions (Excel, PDF or CSV), the file is created on your device and opened in your phone's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it. We never receive a copy.

## Microphone and voice entry
If you use voice entry, the app uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (usually Google's) to turn what you say into text. Depending on your phone's settings, that service may process the audio on the device or on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy. The app itself never records, stores or sends audio; it only receives the text and fills in the form.

## Notifications
If you turn on the daily reminder, budget alerts, bill and subscription reminders or the zakat date reminder, the app shows local notifications on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

## Purchases (Pro)
The app offers a 7-day free trial of Pro features, then an optional one-time Lifetime Pro purchase through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To verify and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (what was bought and when) under RevenueCat's privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy). None of the financial data you enter in the app is shared with RevenueCat. The trial start date is stored only on your device.

## Analytics, ads and tracking
The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs.

## Ask your data
Questions you type or say in Ask your data are answered on your device from the data already stored there. Nothing is sent to us, to an AI service or to anyone else. If you use the microphone button, the phone's speech service is used as described under voice entry.

## Settings stored on the device
Your theme, font and widget data are kept in the app's private storage so the app and its home-screen widgets can show them. They never leave the device.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes all of its data from your device. Backup files you saved yourself remain wherever you put them until you delete them.
